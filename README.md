# Automated Bag Sealing System (ABSS)

Design and proposal for an Automated Bagging and Sealing System, developed during an
internship at **Bejai Ethio Engineering and Industry Solution**, Addis Ababa, Ethiopia,
in partial fulfillment of the internship requirement at **Addis Ababa Science and
Technology University (AASTU)**, Department of Electromechanical Engineering.

## Overview

The system automates the final stage of animal feed packaging — currently a manual,
labor-intensive process — by integrating:

- A **50 kg load cell** weighing unit
- An **auger filler** for precise dispensing
- A **conveyor belt** for bag transfer
- A **continuous band sealer** for heat-sealing 25 kg woven polypropylene (WPP) bags
  laminated with BOPP film
- A **PLC-based control system** to synchronize the whole sequence

Compared to the manual process, the proposed design cuts labor from 3–4 workers to a
single operator and roughly triples throughput (from ~1 bag/min to 3 bags/min).
